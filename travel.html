Getting Around

Traveling in Paris is simple and enjoyable thanks to its efficient Métro system, extensive bus network, and beautiful streets that are perfect for scenic walks between attractions. A single Navigo Easy card can be topped up and used across Métro, bus, and tram lines, making it the easiest way to get around without worrying about individual tickets.

Many of the city's most famous sights are within walking distance of one another, so pairing the Métro for longer hops with walking for the final stretch is often the most rewarding way to experience the city.

Quand Venir

Best Time to Visit

The best times to visit Paris are spring and autumn, when the weather is mild, the city is less crowded, and outdoor cafés feel especially welcoming. Summer brings long, sunny evenings but also the biggest crowds at major landmarks, while winter offers a quieter, softly lit city and festive markets around the holidays.

Whatever the season, packing layers and comfortable walking shoes will serve you well — Parisian weather can shift quickly, and the city is best explored on foot.

Savoir-Vivre

Local Etiquette & Safety

To speak like a local, remember polite phrases such as "Bonjour" and "Merci," and always greet shopkeepers before asking questions or ordering food — it is considered good manners and is often met with a warmer welcome in return.

For safety, keep your belongings secure in busy tourist areas and stay alert near popular landmarks, especially when using public transport during peak hours.
